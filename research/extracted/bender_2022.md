# Bender et al., 2022 - Supportive strategies for nonverbal communication with persons living with dementia: a scoping review

- **Citation**: Bender, E. N., Savundranayagam, M. Y., Murray, L., & Orange, J. B. (2022). Supportive strategies for nonverbal communication with persons living with dementia: a scoping review. *International Journal of Nursing Studies* (article 104365).
- **DOI / PMID**: 10.1016/j.ijnurstu.2022.104365 / PMID not recorded
- **Study design**: Scoping review; CINAHL, PsycInfo, Scopus and PubMed searched on 8 December 2020; 16 empirical studies included.
- **Parameter(s) extracted**: none. The review is about the nonverbal strategies *care partners* use (eye contact, gestures, facial expression, touch, close proximity, frontal orientation) and whether they support communication. It reports no rates of nonverbal behavior by the person with dementia, so it cannot inform `non_verbal_cue_probability`.
- **Notes / caveats**: The authors note that disagreement exists over what counts as "supportive" and that person-centered outcomes are limited. Possible design use: the six strategy categories are a reference for how trainee-side nonverbal supports could be described or evaluated, not for patient priors.
- **Confidence**: not applicable to the priors.
